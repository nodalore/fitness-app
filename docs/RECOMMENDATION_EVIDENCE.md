# Recommendation evidence register — rules-2.0

Reviewed October 18, 2026. Healthy-adult education, not individualized coaching or medical advice. The software does not measure strength, movement skill, fatigue, or recovery. An “advanced” profile changes exercise ranking and starting workload, never assigns a weight or proves a person needs more volume.

## Exact app choices

- Requested 2/3 sessions: Full Body A/B or A/B/C. Requested 4: Upper A/Lower A/Upper B/Lower B. Experienced 5: Upper/Lower/Push/Pull/Legs. Experienced 6: Push/Pull/Legs A/B. Beginners requesting more than three receive an explicitly labeled three-session starting alternative.
- Beginner working sets stay at 1–2 per exercise, normally 8–12 reps. Experienced strength main lifts use 4–8 reps; other main lifts 8–12, accessories 10–15. Main-lift defaults may reach four sets for advanced users. No assigned loads, maximal tests, mandatory failure, or specialist athletic programming.
- Weekly pattern budgets: beginner lower/standard 2/4; experienced general fitness 4/6; intermediate strength/hypertrophy 4/6; advanced strength 6/8 and hypertrophy 6/10. Accessory patterns generally use 2/4. Divide budgets across occurrences with per-exercise caps of 2/3/4 by experience and at least one set per occurrence. Actual totals, including cap/rounding differences, are saved and visible by primary muscle. These are transparent starting choices, not mandatory scientific thresholds. Secondary muscle involvement is not counted as direct sets.
- Rest defaults use catalog ranges (usually 120–180 seconds for compounds, 60–120 for accessories); experienced strength compounds allow at least 180 seconds. Recover longer when needed.
- A/B selection uses occurrence within a workout family and the top suitable candidates. Main strength anchors can stay fixed with B order/emphasis changes. Loaded candidates take priority for experienced users where available. Fewer candidates mean repeated anchors, with a visible limitation.
- New plans have null weekdays and meaningful ordered names. Recovery advice asks users to place rest days as needed and continue order after a missed day, not double sessions. No completion tracker or automated progression is implemented in Stage 3.

## Catalog provenance and limits

`src/data/catalog-expansion.json` defines 18 additive exercises, seven publication records and 18 directional alternatives. `scripts/generate-catalog-migration.mjs` deterministically generates migration 005. Production recommendations only use records actually loaded from Supabase; JSON entries never masquerade as installed rows.

New entries fill loadable squat, unilateral leg, vertical pull, knee-flexion, calf, arm/shoulder accessory, floor-press and band-equipment gaps. Citations support programming/equipment principles; they do not prove each exact cue, diagram, pairing or exercise is universally superior. Technique notes are original general educational cues and cannot replace instruction or a machine manual. Every addition includes requirements, primary/secondary muscle mapping, a loading convention, rest defaults and an explained alternative. Existing IDs, source links, exercises, alternatives, saved plans and histories are not rewritten.

Band entries use the existing catalog loading type `external`, with explicit notes to record band identity/setup rather than invented kilogram tension. Any future logger must honor this distinction. No band-load logging is implemented here.

Exercise illustrations are original inline SVG movement schematics, clearly labeled as general patterns rather than exercise photographs or technique demonstrations. No third-party exercise imagery is downloaded or embedded.
